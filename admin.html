PLANNING

Resource planning

Who works on which project, at what FTE, and for which months. Plan vs actual and the Hub timeline are based on this. Leave To on "ongoing" for open-ended allocations. To change an FTE from a given month, end the old row and add a new one, so past months keep their plan. Opportunities count as tentative.


Import allocations from CSV

Columns needed: Person, Project (an implementation, service delivery project or opportunity), FTE (0,5 or 50%), From (month or date) and optionally To (empty = ongoing).



TIMESHEETS

Import timesheet actuals

Upload the CSV export from your timesheet app. Hours are summed per person, project and month. Importing again overwrites the same person/project/month; to replace a month completely, delete it first.

Columns needed: Employee, Project, Date (or month) and Hours. Separator ; , or tab. Decimals 7,8 or 7.8.
